Reading the range
The three sales span about ฿250M to ฿440M per rai. That is well above Colliers' ฿40–100M per rai band for Kamala in Table 5, so the Patong beachfront evidence should be presented as its own prime-frontage tier, not blended into island averages. Values per rai differ by parcel size and frontage; the smaller Beach Road plot carries the highest rate.

Reading guide

Evidence grades and known gaps

Known gaps
  • Few Phuket hotel sales publish a price; many are off-market.
  • Land-office transfer values often differ from true prices.
  • Patong land sales in Table 4 are broker-reported and not yet matched to transfer documents.
  • Buyer identity is missing for the ibis Kata sale.
  • Two Table 6 lines (ORI portfolio, Phuket luxury hotel) need source documents.
